So, Blogging
============

Being honest, I've been on the internet for a while, and I've been on youtube for about 7 years now.
Somehow I never started a blog, blogging is such a weird concept. Probably why twitter never stuck
for me, I just don't feel the urge to talk about what I'm doing all that much.

That's not to say I don't talk about what I'm doing, I talk a lot. But not in the, more traditional
sit-down and write a letter sense.

What Now?
=========

Well, I'll probably never touch the blog portion of my website again. The only reason I made this was
because it felt wrong to have a website feed without a blog of some kind. Who knows, maybe the whole thing
will finally click and I'll make 60 blog entries.

How does one end a blog?
